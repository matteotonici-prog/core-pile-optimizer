In app.py, add this checkbox near the result plot controls:

show_kern = st.checkbox("Show pile-group kern", value=True)

Then change the plotting call from:

st.plotly_chart(make_figure(s, lc, limits), use_container_width=True)

to:

st.plotly_chart(
    make_figure(s, lc, limits, show_kern=show_kern),
    use_container_width=True,
)
